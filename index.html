    Amounts are solved exactly with the Bateman equations for every branch of the chain. Tiny values that fall below double-precision noise are shown as zero.

    Data: IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides ground states (ENSDF / NUBASE), plus the isomers Pa-234m, Ba-137m and Tc-99m. Helium and other emitted particles are assumed to leave the sample, so mass shares count heavy atoms only.
